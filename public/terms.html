Section 01

About ShareSphere

ShareSphere is a peer-to-peer item rental platform that connects lenders (item owners) with borrowers (renters) across India. ShareSphere verifies the identity of lenders and borrowers, handles booking and payment through a secure escrow process, confirms handover and return with one-time codes and photographs, and provides a dispute resolution service.

The rental itself is an agreement between the lender and the borrower. ShareSphere does not own, inspect, or take physical possession of any listed item.

By registering on ShareSphere — whether as a lender or borrower — you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions in full. If you do not agree, you must not use the platform.

Prototype notice: ShareSphere currently runs as an academic project. Payments are processed through Razorpay in test mode, so no real money is charged or paid out, and transfers to lenders are simulated and recorded in each booking's ledger.

Section 03

Lender Obligations

As a lender on ShareSphere, you agree to:

  • Only list items that you legally own or have explicit authority to rent out
  • Accurately describe the item's condition, defects, and included accessories
  • Provide a minimum of 3 clear photographs and 1 working-condition video for every listing
  • Provide valid payout details (bank account or UPI) before you hand over an item, because rent cannot be released to you without them
  • Hand over the item at the agreed pickup time and confirm the pickup in the app using the borrower's code and a photograph of the item
  • Keep your listing details (availability, price, deposit, condition) up to date
False or misleading item descriptions are grounds for immediate listing removal and account suspension.

Section 05

Booking & Payments

Bookings are made through the platform for the dates you select. Dates that are already booked cannot be selected again. The rent and refundable security deposit are shown on the booking page before you pay.

  • Payment is made online through Razorpay. The rent and the deposit are charged together as one payment
  • After payment is verified, the amount is held in escrow by ShareSphere and the booking is confirmed
  • A booking that is not paid is not confirmed, and its dates are released automatically if payment is not completed within 15 minutes
  • Rent is released to the lender only after pickup is confirmed (see Section 6)
  • Every payment, release, and refund on a booking is recorded in that booking's transaction ledger, which both parties can view
Section 06

Pickup & Return

Handover and return are confirmed in the app so that both parties have a record of the item's condition.

  • Pickup: after payment, the borrower receives a pickup code that only the borrower can see. When the borrower receives the item, the lender enters this code and uploads a photograph of the item. The rent is then released to the lender
  • Return: when the item comes back, the lender enters a return code shown only to the borrower and uploads a photograph of the item. The security deposit is then refunded to the borrower
  • The photographs taken at pickup and at return are stored with the booking and may be used as evidence in a dispute
Never share your code before you have the item in your hands, or after the handover has gone wrong. A code is your confirmation that the handover happened.
Section 07

Cancellations & Refunds

  • A booking that has not been paid can be abandoned at any time. It is cancelled and nothing is charged
  • After payment, the app does not offer a self-service cancellation. If the lender does not hand over the item, the rent is not released to the lender, because release requires the pickup code and photograph
  • In that case the borrower should contact support with the booking ID. A ShareSphere administrator reviews the booking and arranges a refund of the amount held in escrow
  • When the item is returned and confirmed in good condition, the full security deposit is refunded to the borrower
  • If the item is returned damaged or a dispute is raised, the deposit is refunded after deductions decided under Section 8
Section 08

Disputes

Either the lender or the borrower can raise a dispute on a booking after pickup has been confirmed. To raise a dispute, you must describe the problem and may attach a photograph as evidence.

  • Once a dispute is raised, the booking is marked as disputed and the normal return process is paused
  • A ShareSphere administrator reviews the dispute together with the pickup and return photographs and the booking ledger
  • The administrator decides on a deduction from the security deposit, which can never be more than the deposit amount
  • The remaining deposit is refunded to the borrower, and the dispute and decision are recorded with the booking

Disputes are meant to be settled through this process. Nothing in it takes away any legal rights users have under Indian law.

Section 10

Reviews & Ratings
